Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know regarding legal enforceability, stamp duty, and eSign rules.

Is a digital rental agreement legally valid in Indian courts?
↓
Yes, 100%. Digital rental agreements with official state e-Stamp and Aadhaar-based eSign are legally valid and enforceable under the Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 10A) and the Indian Stamp Act, 1899. They carry the exact same evidentiary standing as a traditional paper contract notarized before an advocate.
Does the tenant need to download the ZapDocs app or create an account?
↓
No. ZapDocs features a seamless "Guest Signer" architecture. When the owner executes the agreement, the tenant receives a secure, encrypted token link over WhatsApp and SMS. The tenant can review the entire agreement in any standard mobile or desktop browser and eSign via Aadhaar OTP in under a minute without downloading anything.
How does eStamping work across different states?
↓
ZapDocs integrates directly with official state treasury and e-Stamping portals (such as Stock Holding Corporation of India Limited - SHCIL). Depending on your property location (Karnataka, Maharashtra, Delhi, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, etc.), our system generates the non-judicial stamp certificate with a unique verifiable Government Receipt Number (GRN) and cryptographic QR code.
Can I use this agreement for passport, bank, and visa address proof?
↓
Yes. Government departments, banks (for account opening and credit cards), passport offices, and telecom providers accept certified eStamped rental agreements with Aadhaar eSign verification trails.
What if I need custom clauses for pets, parking, or commercial usage?
↓
Our drafting engine supports full clause customization. You can choose from pre-vetted legal clauses or type in specific mutual arrangements regarding security deposits, painting deductions, maintenance responsibilities, lock-in periods, and termination notice rules.
